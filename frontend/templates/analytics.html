Key Market Insights

Gandhinagar's real estate market is largely driven by location, family-oriented housing demand, and value-conscious pricing. While premium corridors such as Gift City command substantial premiums, buyers can still find attractive opportunities in emerging and affordable localities.

Gandhinagar leans affordable-to-premium

41% of listings fall in the affordable segment, while 37% belong to the premium category. Luxury homes account for only 22% of the market.

Gift City is the premium hotspot

Gift City records the highest average price per square foot, exceeding ₹16,000 per sq.ft., and stands well above other localities.

Kalol and Dahegam are budget-friendly

With prices around ₹3,200–₹4,000 per sq.ft., these areas are strong choices for buyers seeking affordability.

Activity is concentrated in key areas

Localities such as Sargasan, Kudasan, Gift City, Randesan, and Vavol dominate the market, offering buyers the widest range of choices.

Prices vary significantly across Gandhinagar

The heat map reveals substantial differences in price per square foot, highlighting the importance of locality when making purchasing decisions.

Larger homes generally cost more

While property prices tend to increase with built-up area, homes of similar sizes can have very different prices depending on where they are located.

Family-sized homes dominate demand

2 BHK and 3 BHK properties make up the majority of listings, indicating strong demand from family homebuyers.

Most properties are fairly priced

About 81% of listings align closely with the model's estimated value, suggesting that the Gandhinagar market is relatively efficient.

Opportunities remain for informed buyers

Approximately 9% of listings appear attractively priced, while 10% are listed above estimated value, creating opportunities for negotiation and value discovery.

Budget expectations vary by home type

Typical pricing ranges are: 2 BHK affordable below ₹65L and premium between ₹65L–₹90L; 3 BHK affordable below ₹80L and premium between ₹80L–₹1.2Cr; 4 BHK affordable below ₹1.3Cr and premium between ₹1.3Cr–₹1.8Cr.
